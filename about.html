OUR HERITAGE & MISSION

REDEFINING THE MOBILE
DETAILING EXPERIENCE

VANTA was founded to bring concours-level automotive rejuvenation directly to your private garage, driveway, or workplace.

Traditional mobile valeting too often relies on abrasive brushes, recycled harsh water, and hurried techniques that leave fine scratches and hologram swirls in your clear coat. We inverted this entire model.

By outfitting specialized mobile studio vans with multi-stage deionization filtration (producing 0 TDS mineral-free water), temperature-regulated steam extractors, and calibrated dual-action polishers, we deliver true studio-grade paint correction and protection anywhere.
